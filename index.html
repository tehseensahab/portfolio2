If you sell something people reorder, this is for you

Most retention problems in ecommerce aren't a Klaviyo problem — they're a timing and data problem.

Find out which one is costing you most

  1. Flows built on a generic clock

    A 30-day post-purchase sequence doesn't know if your product runs out in 3 weeks or 3 months. I build reorder timing around actual usage, not a template default.

  2. Rising ad costs, flat repeat rate

    If new-customer acquisition does all the work, margins shrink every quarter. I build the retention and subscription layer that takes pressure off ad spend.

  3. No real visibility into what's working

    I run structured A/B tests and segment by behavior, not just purchase date, so decisions come from data — not a hunch about the next email.

  4. Subscribers who quietly churn

    Save flows, flexible cadence options and win-back sequences timed to when someone is actually at risk of cancelling — not after they already have.
